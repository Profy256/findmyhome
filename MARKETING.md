**For buyers:** Browse thousands of verified listings across Kampala, Wakiso, Entebbe, Jinja, Mbarara, Gulu, and beyond. Every seller is identity-verified. Every land listing comes with a title document. Book a property and pay securely online.

**For sellers and agents:** Get a seller dashboard, lead tracking, WhatsApp lead capture, property analytics, and a verified badge — everything you need to close deals faster and build a professional brand online.

**Built for Uganda. Built for trust.**

## 8. FAQ — ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS

**"How much does it cost to list?"**
Creating a seller account and listing your first property is free. We earn a small commission when you close a deal — 5% for individual sellers, 3% for registered companies.

**"Is it only for Kampala?"**
No. We cover 10 districts across Uganda: Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono, Entebbe, Jinja, Mbarara, Gulu, Masaka, Mbale, and Fort Portal. We're expanding.

**"Why do I need to verify my identity?"**
Identity verification protects buyers. Fraud is one of the biggest problems in Uganda's property market. When sellers are verified, buyers trust you faster and leads convert at a higher rate. It actually helps you close deals.

**"How long does KYC verification take?"**
Most verifications complete within minutes thanks to our AI-powered document check. Some take up to 24 hours if manual review is required.

**"What documents do I need for KYC?"**
Individual sellers: National ID, Passport, or Driver's Licence.  
Companies: Business Registration Certificate from URSB + Director's ID.

**"How does the lead dashboard work?"**
Your seller dashboard shows you total leads, WhatsApp leads, and today's leads for each listing in real time. You can see which properties are performing and which need attention.

**"What is the Blue Badge?"**
The Blue Badge is a premium verified status available for UGX 300,000. It appears on your profile and listings, signalling to buyers that you are a serious, professional seller.
